Before you ask

Ourika Valley & Atlas Mountains tours: common questions

Answers about day trips from Marrakech, the Setti Fatma waterfalls, hiking with a local mountain guide and private experiences.

Inclusions depend on the experience you choose, for example a guided Atlas trek, a Setti Fatma waterfalls hike or a Berber villages walk. Before you book, we confirm clearly what is and is not included for your chosen day trip. Message us on WhatsApp with the experience and your date.

The Ourika Valley is a day trip from Marrakech. Exact start and return times depend on the route, the pace of your group and the season, so your guide agrees them with you when you book.

The Setti Fatma waterfalls hike follows mountain paths with some uneven and rocky ground. Tell us your experience level and who is coming, including children or older travellers, and we will advise which Ourika hiking route fits your group.

Yes. Private experiences with a local guide are available for couples, families and small groups. Send us your date, group size and interests, such as hiking, waterfalls, Berber villages or culture, and we will shape the day with you.

Wear comfortable walking shoes with good grip and bring water, sun protection, a hat and a light extra layer, as it can be cooler in the mountains. A small backpack and a camera are useful. We can share a full checklist once your trip is confirmed.

Yes. Your day is led by Mohamed Oudra, a local guide in the Ourika Valley and High Atlas, who knows the trails, the villages and the area around Setti Fatma.

Yes. We offer an Atlas trek and Berber villages experience, and we can include village visits and local culture in a private day trip. Tell us what you would like to see when you contact us.
