The day in short

Leave the Marrakech medina for the Ourika Valley and start the day with breakfast in a Berber village. Then walk the valley with your local guide, at an easy pace, with time for the views and the people who live here.

The day ends with lunch in the village, a Berber village experience shared with a local family.

Trip highlights

  • Berber village breakfast

    Start the morning in the village, before the walk.

  • Guided valley walk

    Tour the Ourika Valley on foot with a local guide.

  • Berber village lunch

    Eat lunch as part of the Berber village experience.

Day by day

Your day starts in the Marrakech medina. From there you travel to a village in the Ourika Valley. Exact pickup time is confirmed with you on WhatsApp.

Stop in the village for breakfast before heading out on the trail.

Walk and tour the valley with your local guide, at a moderate level.

Have lunch in the Berber village as part of the experience, then return to Marrakech.

Included

  • Local guide
  • Breakfast in the village
  • Lunch, Berber village experience

What to bring

  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Water and sun protection
  • A light layer for the mountains
